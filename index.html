Weld-Shaving Robot — R&D Robotics Project

Robotics · Mechanical Design · Controls Team of 4 · 2023

Design and engineering of an automated robot for weld seam finishing, developed as a team-based R&D project with focus on mechanical robustness, force control, and manufacturability.

Key Contributions & Results

  • Designed the complete mechanical hull in Autodesk Inventor, optimised for stiffness, accessibility, and component integration.
  • Defined force and load diagrams for tool–surface interaction, enabling consistent weld removal without damaging base material.
  • Selected motors, actuators, and electronic components based on torque, speed, and duty-cycle constraints.
  • Reduced estimated tool vibration by ~20% through structural layout and mass distribution choices.

Engineering KPIs

  • ±0.5 mm tool positioning tolerance
  • Continuous operation duty cycle > 80%
  • 4-person multidisciplinary collaboration
  • Design-to-prototype in < 4 months

Key discussion point: balancing force control vs mechanical simplicity under industrial constraints.
